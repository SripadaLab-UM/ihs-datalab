diary related to Fitbit sleep minutes on the same night?

- Unit of analysis: participant-night.
- Join the diary to the Fitbit sleep table on participant and date.
- Report how many diary nights have no Fitbit record.
- Look within person (each intern's nights against their own average), not
  only across people.
