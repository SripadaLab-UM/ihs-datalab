## Script and storyboard

---

### Chapter 1: This video covers

**Shot 1.1**

- **Visual:** The title and agenda.
  - agenda · Start a conversation · "starting a conversation"
  - agenda · Ask a question · "asking a question"
  - agenda · Watch it work · "watching the agent work"
  - agenda · Approve its plan · "approving its plan"
- **Narration:**
  > This video shows how to work with DataLab's AI agent in the Workspace.
  > First, starting a conversation, and asking a question. Then, watching
  > the agent work, and approving its plan before it looks at the data.

---

### Chapter 2: Start a conversation

**Shot 2.1**

- **Visual:** New conversation; the modes; Analysis chosen; the note; Start.
  - `modes` · "choose what you'd like to do" · What you'd like to do
  - `note` · "The note underneath" · A data session
  - `none` · "press Start"
- **Narration:**
  > Press New conversation, and choose what you'd like to do. Analysis is
  > for scientific questions: the agent plans first, runs a small pilot, and
  > asks before a large run. The note underneath says this is a data
  > session, with the database and no web. Then press Start.

---

### Chapter 3: Ask a question

**Shot 3.1**

- **Visual:** The question typed and sent.
  - `composer` · "Ask the way" · Your question
  - `none` · "finds them"
- **Narration:**
  > Ask the way you'd ask a colleague: which cohort, what to measure, and
  > what it's for. You don't need to know the table names. The agent finds
  > them.

---

### Chapter 4: Watch it work

**Shot 4.1**

- **Visual:** The agent's steps appearing as it works (sped up); one opened.
  - `step` · "Press plus" · What it saw
  - `none` · "the queries it ran"
- **Narration:**
  > Each step appears as a line, in plain words: the lab's guides it reads,
  > the tables it looks up, and the queries it runs. Press plus on a line
  > to see exactly what the agent saw, like the queries it ran.

---

### Chapter 5: Approve its plan

**Shot 5.1**

- **Visual:** The plan card: its kind, its sections; one edited.
  - `card` · "proposes an analysis plan" · Its analysis plan
  - `kind` · "which kind of analysis" · The kind of analysis, and why
  - `field` · "Edit any section" · Edit any section
- **Narration:**
  > Before it looks at outcome data, the agent proposes an analysis plan,
  > and waits for you. It says which kind of analysis it chose, and why.
  > Edit any section in place, or add one.

**Shot 5.2**

- **Visual:** Approve plan pressed; the plan folds to "frozen".
  - `approve` · "press Approve plan" · Approve plan
  - `frozen` · "It's frozen" · Frozen
- **Narration:**
  > When you're happy, press Approve plan. It's frozen, with the time and a
  > fingerprint, and later work is labelled as following the plan, or as
  > exploratory.

**Shot 5.3**

- **Visual:** The pilot runs (sped up); the agent's answer, asking whether to
  go ahead.
  - `answer` · "stops and asks" · Its answer, and a question for you
- **Narration:**
  > Then the agent runs a pilot: the smallest meaningful run, on part of
  > the data. It reports what it found, and how big the full run would be,
  > then stops and asks whether to go ahead.

**Shot 5.4**

- **Visual:** The end card.
- **Narration:**
  > Next: reading the answer, and the files it made. There's more in Help,
  > under Ask your first question.
