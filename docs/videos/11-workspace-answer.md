### Chapter 2: The answer and its checks

**Shot 2.1**

- **Visual:** The answer, then Checks on this answer.
  - `answer` · "labelled Answer" · The answer
  - `checks` · "Checks on this answer" · How it was checked
  - `none` · "its own work"
- **Narration:**
  > The agent's answer sits under a single rule, labelled Answer. Below it,
  > Checks on this answer says what was checked. First, DataLab's own check
  > of the answer's numbers against what the work produced. Then the rigor
  > review, the agent's check of its own work.

**Shot 2.2**

- **Visual:** A number clicked; its popover.
  - `number` · "is marked" · A number in the answer
  - `popover` · "where it came from" · Where it came from
- **Narration:**
  > Each number in the answer is marked. Click one to see where it came
  > from: the query, the script, or the file that produced it.

**Shot 2.3**

- **Visual:** How this answer was made, opened.
  - `made` · "How this answer was made" · How this answer was made
  - `none` · "the whole story"
- **Narration:**
  > How this answer was made folds up the work behind it: the steps, the
  > queries, the lab guides read, and the files changed. Open it to read
  > the whole story again.

### Chapter 3: The files it made

**Shot 3.1**

- **Visual:** The side panel: Outputs.
  - `panel` · "The side panel" · Outputs
- **Narration:**
  > The side panel keeps the conversation's files. Outputs has what the
  > agent made, like figures, reports and tables, to open and preview.

**Shot 3.2**

- **Visual:** Queries, with Turn this into a workflow; then History.
  - `panel` · "Queries lists" · Every query
  - `workflow` · "turn them into a workflow" · Turn this into a workflow
  - `panel` · "History keeps" · History
- **Narration:**
  > Queries lists every query the agent ran, with its SQL, and can turn
  > them into a workflow. History keeps a checkpoint after every turn, so
  > the files can be put back as they were.
