### Chapter 2: A research session

**Shot 2.1**

- **Visual:** New conversation; Research chosen; its note; Start.
  - `modes` · "Press New conversation" · What you'd like to do
  - `research` · "choose Research" · Research
  - `note` · "The note says" · Web, but no database
  - `none` · "Then press Start"
- **Narration:**
  > For literature, methods or packages, start a research session. Press
  > New conversation, and choose Research. The note says what that means:
  > this agent can use the web, but it has no connection to the study
  > database. Then press Start.

**Shot 2.2**

- **Visual:** The question typed and sent; the agent works (sped up); its
  answer.
  - `composer` · "Ask it anything" · Your question
  - `answer` · "and answers" · Its answer
- **Narration:**
  > Ask it anything you'd look up yourself. Here, which R packages suit
  > mixed models with repeated daily measures. It searches the web, reads,
  > and answers, with its sources.

**Shot 2.3**

- **Visual:** The answer, scrolled.
  - `answer` · "Anything you attach" · A research session
  - `none` · "nothing moves the other way"
- **Narration:**
  > Anything you attach to a research session may reach the internet, so
  > attach only what may go online. Notes and code from it can come into a
  > data session, but nothing moves the other way.

---

### Chapter 3: The research helper

**Shot 3.1**

- **Visual:** New conversation, Data extraction, Start; the question typed
  and sent.
  - `note` · "no web" · Database, but no web
  - `composer` · "Here, in a Data extraction session" · Your question
- **Narration:**
  > A data session has the database, but no web. When its agent needs to
  > look something up, it asks the research helper, and only with your
  > approval. Here, in a Data extraction session, we ask for the standard
  > PHQ-9 severity cutoffs.

**Shot 3.2**

- **Visual:** The agent works (sped up) and pauses with the approval card:
  "The agent wants to look something up online". What will be sent; Send to
  the research helper pressed; the helper works (sped up); its answer.
  - `card` · "and asks you first" · The approval card
  - `sent` · "What will be sent" · Exactly what goes out
  - `edit` · "You can edit it" · Edit the question
  - `decline` · "press Don't send" · Or decline
  - `send` · "Press Send to the research helper" · Send it
  - `none` · "The helper looks it up"
  - `answer` · "on the web, answers" · The helper's answer
- **Narration:**
  > The agent pauses, and asks you first. What will be sent is the exact
  > question, and nothing else: no conversation, no files, no database. You
  > can edit it, or press Don't send. Press Send to the research helper. The
  > helper looks it up on the web, answers, and is then deleted.

**Shot 3.3**

- **Visual:** The agent carries on (sped up) with the helper's answer.
  - `answer` · "web content" · Web content
  - `none` · "aren't counted as traced"
- **Narration:**
  > The agent carries on with the helper's answer. It's web content, so
  > numbers taken from it aren't counted as traced.

**Shot 4.1**

- **Visual:** The end card.
- **Narration:**
  > That's research sessions and the research helper. There's more in Help,
  > under What DataLab will and won't do.
